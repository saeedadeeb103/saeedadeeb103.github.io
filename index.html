Diagnosing & fixing "time-blindness" in VLMs

Studied why vision-language models can't perceive content that exists only in motion, across Qwen2.5-VL (3B/7B) and LLaVA-NeXT (7B). Built a training-free Temporal Decoding Preprocessor — optical flow plus motion clustering — that lets any existing VLM read hidden temporal patterns as a static image. On SpookyBench this raised accuracy to 42.6–55.2% vs. 1.3–4.4% for native-video baselines, confirmed genuinely temporal by a new Temporal Dependency Score (0.80–0.98).
